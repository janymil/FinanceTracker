**Core AI Functions (9 Total)**

**1. Transaction Categorization**

- **`categorizeExpense`** - Automatically categorizes transactions using AI analysis of merchant names and transaction patterns

**2. Financial Tips & Insights**

- **`generateContextualFinancialTips`** - Provides personalized financial advice based on current month's spending, budgets, and goals
- **`generateFinancialTips`** - Generates comprehensive financial analysis with 4-section structured insights (spending patterns, saving recommendations, budget optimization, advanced analysis)
- **`generateSpendingInsights`** - Analyzes expense transactions to identify unusual patterns, optimization opportunities, and spending trends

**3. Financial Health Analysis**

- **`generateAIFinancialHealthScore`** - Calculates personalized financial health scores (0-100) with detailed factor breakdown and AI analysis
- **`generateFinancialHealthScore`** - Alternative financial health scoring system

**4. Predictive Analytics**

- **`generateTrendPredictions`** - Provides AI-powered forecasting for category spending and financial trends
- **`generateExpensePredictions`** - Predicts future expense patterns based on historical data
- **`generateDailyRecommendations`** - Generates daily personalized financial recommendations

**AI Architecture**

**Dual Processing System:**

- **External Fly.io AI Processor** (`external-services/ai-processor/index.ts`) - Stateless OpenAI service
- **Server Fallback** (`server/openai.ts`) - Local OpenAI processing when external service unavailable

**Unified AI Cache System:**

- All functions use `UnifiedAICache` for performance optimization
- TTL values range from 24 hours (daily recommendations) to 168 hours (financial tips)
- User-isolated caching with language-aware keys

**Multilingual Support:**

- All AI functions support English, Spanish, and Slovak
- Language parameters passed through normalized codes (en-us, es-pe, sk-sk)
- AI responses generated in user's preferred language

**Premium Features:**

- Most AI functions are available to Standard+ subscribers
- Basic expense categorization available to all users
- Advanced AI analysis (financial health, predictions) requires Premium subscription
