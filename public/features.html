Getting Started

FinanzerPro is a comprehensive personal finance management application that helps you track expenses, manage budgets, and achieve financial goals.

Key Features

Transaction Management

Record income and expenses with automatic AI categorization. Edit, delete, and organize your financial data with ease.

Receipt OCR Scanning

Use your phone camera to scan receipts. Our OCR technology extracts amount, vendor, and date information automatically.

Mobile QR Upload

Scan QR codes from your desktop to upload receipts directly from your phone camera.

Budget Tracking

Set monthly budgets for different categories and track your spending progress with visual indicators.

Financial Goals

Create savings goals for vacations, emergency funds, or major purchases. Track progress and collaborate with family.

AI Financial Assistant

Get personalized financial tips, expense predictions, and spending insights powered by artificial intelligence.
